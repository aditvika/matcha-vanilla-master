# MV Master

A professional and modern splash screen screen designed for a production app, with a dark charcoal-blue background. At the top-center, there is a large, iconic, clean white 'MV' monogram logo (as seen in image_2.png). Directly beneath the MV logo, the text 'Matcha Vanilla Production' is centered in a clean, soft white font. The lower portion of the screen features the attribution: 'Powered by' in small, soft white text, followed by the colorful 'Gemini AI' text logo with its distinct gradient (as seen in image_2.png). The overall atmosphere is premium and intelligent, with a very subtle, soft gradient light effect emanating from the text and logos, giving them a slight glow against the dark background. The single sparkle icon from the corner is retained. The visual style is minimal yet crafted
